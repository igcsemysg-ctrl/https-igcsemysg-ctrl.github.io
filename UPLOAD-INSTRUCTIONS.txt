What changes:

- The homepage no longer contains the interactive assessment.
- Assessment buttons now open assessment.html.
- assessment.html contains the complete 15-question diagnostic.
- A learning workspace section explains resumable work, progress, assignment timing and goals.
- The layout remains responsive for desktop, tablet and mobile.
- A free resource library is available from the main navigation.
- The placeholder revision checklists have been removed.
- Resources can be added manually with title, subject, board, type, topics,
  syllabus code, dates, description, file details and custom order.
- Search, subject/type/board filters and three arrangement options are included.
- The navigation separates its content groups with subtle visual dividers.
- The misleading green status dot beside Learning system has been removed.
- The main navigation now uses Enquire as its focused right-side action.
- The full enquiry form is available on enquiry.html instead of the homepage.
- The enquiry page supports prepared attachments, email and formatted WhatsApp routes.
- learning-system.html explains the LMS through a visual learning record,
  connected workflow and role-specific benefits.
- Learning-system buttons open https://app.igcsemysg.site.
- A member-only resource preview redirects to app.igcsemysg.site/pricing.
- The careers application opens only when requested.
- Applicants can prepare CV and cover-letter files before choosing email or
  WhatsApp.
- Email applications are addressed to igcsemysg@gmail.com.
- The homepage and important pages now include technical search metadata.
- robots.txt and sitemap.xml help search engines discover the public pages.

After upload:

1. Commit all uploaded and replaced files to the main branch.
2. Wait one to three minutes for GitHub Pages to deploy.
3. Open the homepage in a private browser window.
4. Test the Take the assessment button and complete the 15 questions once.
5. Open resources.html and test search, filters and arrangements.
6. Open learning-system.html and test every platform button.
7. Test the membership button at the bottom of the resources page.
8. Open careers.html and confirm Start application expands the form.
9. Open enquiry.html and test both email and WhatsApp enquiry routes.
10. Complete a test application and verify both career submission routes.
11. Open robots.txt and sitemap.xml from the live domain.
12. Follow SEO-SETUP-GUIDE.txt to verify Google Search Console and submit the sitemap.

Keep assessment.html in the same folder as index.html so the redirect works.
Keep resources.html, resources-data.js and resources.js together in the same
folder.

Read RESOURCE-MANAGEMENT-GUIDE.txt before adding PDFs, worksheets or links.
