Enquiry & student fit assessment

Before deciding on tuition, let us first understand what is happening.

If the concerns described throughout our website feel familiar, you do not need to choose a programme immediately. Start by telling us about the student’s situation so we can determine whether we are genuinely the right fit to help.

How the process works

An enquiry begins a conversation—not an enrolment.

Each stage reduces uncertainty before any decision is made.

02WE LEARN MORE

03FURTHER DISCUSSION

Make an informed decision

Together, we consider suitable support, realistic progress and whether our approach aligns.

What we want to understand

The same result does not always mean the same problem.

Two students receiving the same grade may require completely different support. Before deciding what may be appropriate, we need to understand what is actually happening.

An honest fit assessment

We do not believe every student should automatically join us.

The required support may fall outside our expertise. The desired outcome may not be realistic within the available time. A different intervention may be more suitable, or our approach may not fit the student.

If we do not believe we can realistically provide the support or outcome you expect, we would rather be honest before asking you to commit.
We would rather have an honest conversation than enrol a student into something unlikely to be right for them.
