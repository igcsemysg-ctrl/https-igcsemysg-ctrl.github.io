The support framework

From uncertainty to a clearer direction.

The diagnostic is only a starting signal. Teaching decisions are refined through conversation, observation and the student’s response to support.

01

Consult

Understand concerns, goals, learning history and the student’s current sense of direction.

02

Troubleshoot

Separate likely gaps, misconceptions, language barriers, exam habits and motivational concerns.

03

Trial

Test focused explanations and short tasks to see what produces a meaningful response.

04

Act

Build lessons, practice and routines around the strongest evidence—not assumptions.

05

Refine

Review understanding and performance, then adjust until the next direction is clearer.

Responsible scope: tutors can identify educational and motivational patterns, but do not diagnose psychological conditions. Families should seek an appropriately qualified professional when concerns extend beyond tuition.

Useful details

Clear answers before you begin.

For a question specific to the student, send the syllabus, subject and current concern through the enquiry section.

01Which pathways are supported?+

Cambridge IGCSE, Pearson Edexcel IGCSE and Singapore O-Level pathways.

02What happens before tuition begins?+

We first discuss the student’s goals, learning history and current concerns. The diagnostic can provide an initial direction, but the tutor still verifies the barrier through conversation and trial work.

03Is the diagnostic a graded test?+

No. It is a self-reflection screening that highlights likely struggle patterns. It does not measure intelligence, predict grades or replace an academic consultation.

04Can lessons support schoolwork and examinations?+

Yes. Lessons can address immediate school needs while remaining aligned with longer-term syllabus and examination priorities.

05How is progress reviewed?+

Through discussion, worked questions, practice and feedback. The teaching focus changes when the evidence points to a different barrier.
