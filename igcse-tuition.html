Start with the real need

IGCSE tuition should address the reason progress has slowed.

Two students with the same grade may need very different support. One may have gaps in foundational knowledge; another may understand the topic but misread command words, leave working unclear or run out of time. A third may know what to do but struggle to maintain a consistent study routine.

That is why the starting point matters. Useful tuition combines the student’s declared current and target grades with schoolwork, attempted questions, tutor observation and the student’s own account of what feels difficult. Early conclusions should remain provisional until there is enough evidence.

01

Concept understanding

Check whether ideas are understood well enough to explain and apply.

02

Exam application

Review interpretation, method, working, timing and answer structure.

03

Language demands

Separate subject knowledge from difficulty understanding or expressing an answer.

04

Study direction

Clarify priorities when revision feels inconsistent, unfocused or overwhelming.

Syllabus alignment

“IGCSE” is not one identical course.

The board, subject specification, syllabus code and examination route shape what should be taught and how answers are assessed.

CAMBRIDGE

Cambridge IGCSE

Support should follow the student’s exact syllabus and paper route, including the relevant content boundaries, assessment objectives and question style.

Bring to the consultation
  • Subject and syllabus code
  • Core or Extended route, where relevant
  • Recent paper, schoolwork or topic list
PEARSON EDEXCEL

Pearson Edexcel International GCSE

Teaching should be connected to the correct specification and paper structure rather than relying on a general topic label alone.

Bring to the consultation
  • Exact subject specification
  • Paper or tier information, where relevant
  • Recent feedback and target grade

A directed process

From uncertainty to useful academic action.

The sequence is simple: understand the problem, test the explanation, teach what matters and review evidence of change.

  1. 01

    Consult

    Clarify the board, subject, recent performance, target and the student’s present concern.

  2. 02

    Diagnose carefully

    Use declared results, attempted work, short questions and observation to form an initial learning profile.

  3. 03

    Rebuild and apply

    Address misconceptions, then connect improved understanding to syllabus-specific examination tasks.

  4. 04

    Record and refine

    Keep priorities, assignments, expected duration, feedback and goals visible between lessons.

Questions families ask

Choosing IGCSE tuition with clearer expectations.

These answers explain the role of tuition before you decide whether it fits the student’s current need.

01What is IGCSE tuition?+

IGCSE tuition is additional academic support aligned with an International General Certificate of Secondary Education subject and examination board. Effective support should connect concept teaching, syllabus coverage and exam application to the student’s current evidence and goal.

02Is Cambridge IGCSE the same as Pearson Edexcel International GCSE?+

No. They are separate examination pathways with their own specifications, paper structures and assessment requirements. The exact board and syllabus or specification should be confirmed before lessons are planned.

03Can online IGCSE tuition be one-to-one?+

Yes. IGCSEMYSG uses a one-to-one online format so the tutor can adjust pace, explanation and practice around one student’s questions and current priorities.

04How do you decide what the student should work on first?+

We begin with the student’s stated concern, current and target grades, previous work and short diagnostic evidence. The tutor then checks those initial signals through questions and trial work before treating them as a reliable teaching priority.

05Does tuition include exam practice?+

Exam-style work is introduced alongside understanding. The balance depends on the student’s stage: some need foundations rebuilt first, while others need more work on timing, interpretation, method or answer structure.

06How can a parent enquire?+

Send the student’s year level, examination board, subject, current concern and any known syllabus code through the enquiry form. It opens a prepared WhatsApp message so the conversation can continue directly.
