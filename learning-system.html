The learning cycle

One connected route from assignment to improvement.

The system is designed to reduce uncertainty at each step—not add another dashboard that students have to decode.

TUTOR

Set the purpose

02
STUDENT

03
SUBMISSION

Keep the evidence

Completed work, timing and submission status form the record used for review.

05
PROGRESS

Refine the plan

A dependable learning record

The system should answer practical questions quickly.

Students should not need to search through messages, files and several pages just to understand what they are meant to do next.

What is required?

Required assignments and tutor priorities are visibly separated from optional resources and extra practice.

02

Where do I submit it?
